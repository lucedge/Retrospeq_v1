# Retrospeq — Marketing Brief

For anyone writing copy, running campaigns, or talking to press and users about Retrospeq.

---

## The one-sentence version

**Retrospeq answers "was this a good decision?" — not "did this trade make money?"**

Every other journal measures outcome and calls it analysis. We measure the decision.

---

## Why that matters

A good trade can lose money. A bad trade can make money. Every trader knows this and no tool acts like it.

So traders end a green week feeling validated when they broke every rule they set, and end a red week feeling broken when they did everything right. The scoreboard they're using measures the wrong thing, and it slowly teaches them the wrong lessons.

**Journaling is the fix everyone knows about and almost nobody does.** It's like working out — the benefit is undisputed and adherence is terrible. That's not a discipline problem. It's a product problem: existing journals demand a lot of typing and give back charts that mostly restate your P&L.

Retrospeq inverts it. Your broker tells us what happened. You tell us what you were thinking. We tell you which of your thinking actually works — and then help you turn that into a rule you can hold yourself to.

---

## Who this is for

**Primary: serious retail forex and crypto traders.** Discretionary, one to five years in, past the beginner phase, actively trying to become consistent. They already suspect their problem is behavioural rather than analytical.

**Primary sub-segment: funded and prop-challenge traders.** They arrive with externally imposed hard rules — daily drawdown, trailing drawdown, consistency limits — and no tool that tracks them properly. They pay $100–600 per challenge attempt. *(Feature ships v1.1 — do not market it before then.)*

**Not for:** algorithmic traders (no decisions to journal), pure investors (timescale too long), or absolute beginners (nothing to analyse yet).

**Geography:** forex and crypto first, globally. India-specific market support comes later.

---

## Positioning

> Retrospeq is a decision journal for traders, not a performance dashboard.

| Category | Them | Us |
|---|---|---|
| Trading journals | Log everything, get charts back | Log what your broker can't see, get answers back |
| Analytics platforms | More metrics | Fewer, and only when they're real |
| Trading psychology apps | Generic mindset content | Your own behaviour, measured |
| Prop firm dashboards | Are you passing? | Are you deciding well? |

**Do not position against journaling.** Position against *outcome-worship*. The enemy isn't spreadsheets — it's the belief that a green month means you traded well.

---

## The three things that are genuinely different

**1. The graduation loop.** Retrospeq finds what works in your trading, then helps you turn it into a rule and tracks whether you keep it — and tells you when that edge stops working. Nobody else closes this loop. Everyone else stops at the chart.

**2. We tell you when we don't know.** "Not enough data yet — 8 more trades on this setup." No competitor says this, because every one of them is incentivised to look busy. It's our most distinctive product behaviour and our strongest trust signal.

**3. It doesn't interrupt you.** No alerts before a trade. No blocking. No "are you sure?" The app shows you where you stand and stays quiet. One notification per week.

---

## Claims we can make

Every one of these is true and defensible in code:

- "Retrospeq never blocks a trade."
- "Your data is never compared against other users." *(No cross-user analytics in v1. Every baseline is your own history.)*
- "We ask for a read-only credential that can't place trades." *(Enforced — we reject credentials capable of trading.)*
- "One notification a week. That's it."
- "We'll tell you when we don't have enough data."
- "Your streak counts weeks you closed the loop, not days you traded."
- "No ads. Ever."

## Claims we must not make

| Don't say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Increase your win rate" / "Trade profitably" | Unsubstantiated financial performance claim. Regulatory risk, and it's the exact outcome-worship we're positioned against |
| "AI-powered coaching" | AI ships in v1.1. Don't sell what isn't there |
| "Detects revenge trading and tilt" *(as a feature name)* | We can use this vocabulary in marketing, but never imply clinical or psychological diagnosis |
| "Track your stop movements" | Not available on history-only broker sync. Verify before promising |
| "Prop firm rule tracking" | **v1.1.** Do not market before it ships |
| "Guaranteed" / "proven" anything | We're a measurement tool, not an edge |

**Financial promotion rules vary by jurisdiction.** Anything implying trading performance improvement needs legal review before it runs, particularly for UK, EU and US audiences.

## Messaging by surface

**Homepage headline candidates:**
- "Was that a good trade, or just a lucky one?"
- "Your P&L doesn't tell you if you traded well."
- "A journal that measures your decisions, not your luck."

**The hook — what a new user sees in their first 60 seconds** (this is also the best demo asset we have):

> Across your last 214 trades, Friday afternoons lost money 68% of the time. Everything else won 54%.

No setup, no data entry. Connect a broker, get told something true about yourself. **Lead every demo with this.**

**Objection handling:**

| "I already journal in a spreadsheet." | Your spreadsheet can't tell you that your conviction ratings actually predict outcomes, or that you re-enter within 90 seconds of a loss eleven times a month. It also can't hold you to anything. |
| "I don't have time for journaling." | Ten seconds before a trade, thirty seconds at the end of the day. Your broker does the rest. |
| "Why not just use my broker's stats?" | Your broker knows what you did. It doesn't know what you were thinking, and it can't tell you which of your thinking works. |
| "Is my data safe?" | Read-only credential that can't place trades. We reject anything that can. Your data is never compared against other users. |

## Pricing story

| Tier | Positioning |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Not a trial — a real product. Import, behaviour findings, three rules, adherence, streak |
| **Pro** | Strategies, judgment findings, the graduation loop, AI insights |
| **Trader+** *(v1.1)* | AI chat and AI-built strategies and rulebooks |

**Free gives away the thesis in miniature.** A free user experiences the actual product — they can see everything about their behaviour, they just can't act on more than three rules. That's a quantity cap, not a capability cap.

The upgrade line writes itself from their own data: *"You're at 3 of 3 rules. Your history suggests four more."*

---

## Tone

**Direct, unhurried, never hyped.** Retrospeq is the calm tool in a category full of neon dashboards and rocket emojis.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "You re-entered within 90 seconds of a loss 11 times." | "🚨 REVENGE TRADING DETECTED" |
| "Not enough data yet." | "Insights coming soon!" |
| "Nothing to close out." | "You're all caught up! 🎉" |
| Show a real number | Show a mockup with invented numbers |

**Never use fabricated screenshots.** Every number in marketing should come from a real anonymised account. The product's whole claim is honesty about data; faking a dashboard would be the worst possible unforced error.

---

## What not to lead with

**Gamification is not the USP.** Nobody switches journals for XP. Streaks and milestones are a retention mechanic — they keep people logging long enough for the graduation loop to have data. Mention them as a feature; never build the pitch on them.

**AI is not the pitch either.** It ships in v1.1 and it narrates findings the statistics engine computes. Selling "AI insights" puts us in a crowded, undifferentiated category. The graduation loop is the story.

---

## Launch sequencing

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Closed beta** | 6–10 full-time traders. Case studies and real screenshots come from here. Do not launch publicly before this cohort has used it for a month |
| **v1 public** | Forex and crypto. Lead with the hook and the graduation loop |
| **v1.1** | Prop firm rulebooks + AI. This is the moment to go hard at the funded-trader segment — and it plausibly supports its own higher tier |
| **v2** | Social and leaderboards, ranked on logging consistency only |

**Channels worth testing:** trading communities where discipline is already the topic; funded-trader forums (ahead of v1.1, for list-building only); YouTube traders who talk about psychology rather than setups; long-form content on the decision-vs-outcome distinction, which is genuinely interesting and under-written.

---

## Two things to protect

**"Not enough data yet" is a feature, not a limitation.** If marketing pressures the product to always show something, we lose the single most distinctive thing about it. Sell the honesty.

**The Clear state — an app that says "nothing to close out" and stops.** It will look like an unfinished screenshot to anyone who hasn't read this brief. It's the strongest visual expression of what makes Retrospeq different from every dashboard in the category.

One sentence

Retrospeq answers “was this a good decision?” — not “did this trade make money?” Every visual rule below is a consequence of that sentence. The mood is instrument black × amber LED: a gauge cluster, not a magazine. Calm, precise, unhurried. The product’s retention hook is “the one honest record of how I actually traded”, so the interface has to feel honest — it never performs excitement it hasn’t earned.

Logo

The q glyph: read once it’s a letter; read twice it’s a target that missed. The ring’s centre is where you aimed; the dot is where it landed. Set alongside Archivo 800 / −.045em for the wordmark. Below ~20px drop the dot and let the q take the accent (favicon is pre-adjusted).

The one hard rule: no red/green, ever, anywhere. Direction is geometry (which side of zero), state is weight and edge. There is deliberately no success/danger pair. --rq-accent-soft and .rq-cost mark a trade-off to weigh, never a warning to dismiss.

Type — two faces, three jobs

Archivo is voice and chrome (display 800 / −.045em; headings 700 / −.032em). Azeret Mono is every number without exception, tabular, plus eyebrow labels. A metric never shares a typeface with an opinion. Both self-hosted, SIL OFL.

Components

Live in brand/css/components.css; every screen in the mockups is built from them. Rules that look like bugs:

RuleWhy
One .rq-btn per viewTwo primaries means the screen has two jobs.
.rq-btn--equal in identical pairsWhere the product says the current state is incoherent but has no opinion on the fix. Refactoring to primary + secondary breaks an ethics decision.
Gauges and the ambient strip always visibleAn indicator that only appears at a limit is an alarm and interrupts at the worst moment.
Ratings are dots, values are steppers, pick-one is pillsNothing on a fast-capture screen may need a keyboard.
.rq-num on every numberTabular figures stop columns jittering; mono marks measurement, not judgement.
Fixed-width slots in rowsTrue vertical lanes; never gap alone.
Status chips carry text“Needs attention” is an ink ring plus a reason and a fix, never a colour.

Motion

Two durations (140ms controls, 220ms state), one ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1), and almost nothing moves. Ambient state crossfades; nothing pulses or shakes. prefers-reduced-motion collapses everything globally. Celebratory motion exists only for the three verified actions in the spec — never for money, streak length or field completeness.

Icons

Inline SVG only, 24-unit viewBox, 2px round strokes, 17–20px, aria-hidden; the four tab icons in instrument.html are the set. No icon library, no emoji, anywhere.

Voice & tone

Direct, unhurried, never hyped. Retrospeq is the calm tool in a category full of neon dashboards and rocket emojis. Two voices in the product: the observer (factual, always on) and the negotiator (only in the weekly review’s decisions). Marketing speaks in the observer’s voice.

Sounds like us

  • “Nothing to close out.”
  • “31 of 34 rules held, up from 27.”
  • “Not enough data yet — 8 more trades on this setup.”
  • “You re-entered within 90 seconds of a loss 11 times.”
  • “Which one is true?”
  • “Free is a real product, not a trial.”

  • “Adherence: 91%”
  • “You’re revenge trading” — any syndrome name
  • “Don’t lose your streak!”
  • “Increase your win rate” — any performance claim
  • “AI-powered insights” as the pitch

Never

  • Red or green, anywhere, for anything
  • A second accent, a gradient, a glow, a drop shadow
  • Currency on the home screen
  • A modal or toast during entry or an open position
  • Confetti, flames, streak warnings, countdown timers
  • A percentage where a ratio belongs
  • Fonts other than Archivo and Azeret Mono
  • An icon library or an emoji
  • A fabricated screenshot or number
  • A performance claim (“improve your win rate”)
  • Naming a syndrome (“revenge trading”, “tilt”, “FOMO”)
  • More than one notification a week
